# Plan

The evergreen context the advisor uses to give grounded advice. Keep it to roughly one page so it stays readable and easy to update by hand. Replace every `[Example: ...]` placeholder with real, specific detail -- numbers, names, dates. While placeholders remain, the advisor treats the plan as not set up.

## Updates

Dated journal of decisions, new constraints, and shifts in thinking. Newest at the top.

- [Example: 2026-01-15 -- Decided to focus Q1 on X instead of Y because Z.]

## Goal

One measurable goal for this year, with a number and a date.

[Example: By the end of 2026, grow the business to $X in annual revenue while keeping meetings under 10 hours a week.]

## This quarter

The single most important focus for this quarter or month.

[Example: Ship the paid product and get the first 50 paying customers.]

## Principles

Three or four rules used to make decisions. These help the advisor flag when a choice drifts from what matters.

- [Example: Go where work feels like play -- do the work that energizes, not just what pays.]
- [Example: Keep the main thing the main thing -- protect the core work from shiny distractions.]
- [Example: Do the simple thing first -- ship early, ship often, learn in public.]
